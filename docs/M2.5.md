# M2.5 The upfront hearing

Public behavior added by this chunk. See
`spec/milestones/M2-commission-and-backbrief.md` §M2.5.

Once the orchestrator's backbrief and the clerk's list are both in the
ledger, `court commission` holds the upfront hearing on the terminal:

1. The orchestrator's restated goal is shown verbatim, in at most
   three lines — the text from the ledger, not the clerk's and not the
   raw commission string.
2. The expected decisions that need a ruling now are asked, at most
   seven, in this order: guarantee categories first, then decisions
   only one list foresaw, then decisions both foresaw. Candidates past
   seven stay open and their count is printed.
3. Every question is yes or no. The agent's intended default is not
   printed until the human has answered that question; it is then
   shown beside the answer, and the human may change the answer.
4. Enter with no answer skips the question: it stays expected and
   open, and no `decision.ruled` is recorded — a skipped question is
   never recorded as an accepted default.
5. Only an actual answer is recorded, as `decision.ruled` (actor
   `human`) naming the decision's projected `D<n>` id, so a later
   matching decision inherits the ruling (`01` §6).

With standard input not a terminal the hearing is skipped, the output
says so, and nothing is ruled.

The hearing output is a deterministic function of the ledger events
and the answers read; the same fixture commission and answers replay
to identical output.
